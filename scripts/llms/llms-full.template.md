## 3. Services in Detail

### 3a. Sales Recruiting (/recruit/sales)

**Scope**: B2B SaaS sales leadership search and placement.

**Roles placed**:
- Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
- Vice President of Sales
- Head of Sales / Director of Sales
- Enterprise Account Executive (AE)
- Mid-Market Account Executive
- Strategic Business Development Representative (BDR/SDR)
- Revenue Operations (RevOps) Director
- Sales Enablement Lead
- Customer Success Leader

**Vetting methodology**:
- Quota attainment verification (specific numbers, time periods, deal types)
- Sales cycle depth and average contract value (ACV) assessment
- Outbound methodology audit (cold calling, sequencing, ABM)
- Behavioral interview for coachability, competitiveness, and resilience
- References from direct managers, peers, and buyers

**Engagement models**: Kas Seat (monthly retainer), Kas Milestone Search (quoted per role), Kas Directed Pursuit, and contingency. Director, VP, and CRO searches are quoted. See Programs & Pricing below. Searches typically take 3 to 8 weeks from intake to offer accepted.

---

### 3b. Fractional Sales Recruiting (/recruit/fractional-sales)

**Scope**: Embedded recruiting partnership for startups and growth-stage companies that need dedicated recruiting bandwidth without the overhead of full retained agency fees.

**Best for**:
- Pre-Series A to Series B companies building their first sales team
- Companies emerging from product-market fit with a defined ICP
- Teams needing to hire 3 to 10 sales reps over a rolling 6 to 12 month period

**How it works**:
- The Kas Group acts as an extension of the founding team or VP of Sales
- Actively manages the top of funnel (sourcing, outreach, initial screen)
- Participates in panel interviews and debrief sessions
- Advises on comp bands, titles, equity expectations, and territory design

**Pricing**: Kas Seat, $5k to $8k/mo, up to $10k/mo for senior AE and above. Zero success fees.

---

### 3c. AI & Data Engineering Recruiting (/recruit/ai)

**Scope**: Placement of AI/ML and Data Engineering talent for AI-native companies and large enterprise AI teams.

**Roles placed**:
- Machine Learning Engineer (MLE)
- ML Operations Engineer (MLOps)
- AI Architect / Principal AI Engineer
- Foundation Model & LLM Engineer
- NLP Researcher / Applied Scientist
- Senior Staff Data Engineer (dbt, Spark, Kafka, Flink, Iceberg)
- Lead Data Scientist / Quantitative Researcher
- Analytics Engineer

**AI Vetting Process**:
1. Resume and portfolio review by Derek Norton
2. Live technical interview (45 to 75 min): algorithm design, system architecture, coding (Python/SQL)
3. Domain-specific deep-dive: statistics, probability, ML system design, or data modeling
4. Practical case study: real-world scenario relevant to the hiring company's stack
5. Written assessment summary delivered to hiring team before final round

**This process filters out**: LLM wrapper builders with no ML fundamentals, data analysts presenting as data scientists, engineers who know tools but cannot reason about systems.
