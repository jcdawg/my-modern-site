## Programs & Pricing

Sales hiring runs through programs. Contingency is still available for sales roles. Full menu: [Programs](https://www.thekasgroup.com/programs). Price detail: [Fractional Sales Recruiting Cost](https://www.thekasgroup.com/guides/fractional-sales-recruiting-cost).

- **Kas Seat**: monthly sales fractional retainer. $5k to $8k/mo, up to $10k/mo for senior AE and above. Zero success fees. Page: https://www.thekasgroup.com/recruit/fractional-sales
- **Kas Milestone Search**: success or retained search, quoted per role. Example fees: SDR/BDR $5k, AE $7.5k, Sr AE / Sales Manager $10k. Paid in milestones: start, qualified candidates delivered, hire.
- **Leadership (Director, VP, CRO)**: quoted. Specialist or retained quote only. Milestone example fees do not apply to leadership roles.
- **Kas Directed Pursuit**: list-driven outbound search. A search returns a list, the client marks who to pursue, and the client owns the list. No published price.
- **Contingency**: still available for sales roles.
- **AI, ML, and data engineering searches**: quoted per role.
